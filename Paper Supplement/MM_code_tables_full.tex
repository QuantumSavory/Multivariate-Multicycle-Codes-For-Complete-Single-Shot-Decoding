\begin{abstract}
Complete versions of Tables II--XI of ``Multivariate Multicycle Codes for Complete Single-Shot Decoding''. The paper lists a selection of these instances. An entry $\leq d$ denotes an upper bound given by the weight of a verified nontrivial logical operator; an entry without $\leq$ denotes an exact distance established by matching upper and lower bounds (Appendix C of the paper and the \texttt{Distance Audit} directory of this repository).
\end{abstract}
